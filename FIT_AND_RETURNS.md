Chandni Lane needs separate attention. It had 146 too-large returns and five too-small returns. On 31 consented kept orders, the average kept size was 0.26 size steps above the published-chart recommendation. The discrepancy between those signals is why the chart is flagged. These are aggregate observations from different subsets, so inspect production measurements and chart application before changing the chart.

The category table uses the mean chart-to-kept gap. A chart is flagged when at least 15 consented kept orders support an absolute gap of 0.55 steps or more, or a gap of at least 0.20 steps conflicts with a return imbalance of at least 12 percentage points. The rule flags seven women's kurta brand charts. The brief's eight-brand figure uses its own synthetic setup.
