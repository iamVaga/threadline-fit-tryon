# Image credits

FitCheck uses original vector garment illustrations for the synthetic catalogue. They are navigation aids. Live try-on requires a garment-only photo mapped to the exact SKU or an upload of a flat-lay or mannequin photograph that the operator may use.

The Wikimedia photos from the previous demo were removed. No photographs or render examples are bundled. A gallery image needs explicit public display permission in addition to consent to process the source photo.

Free sample catalogue: 15 clothing items (dresses, tops, men's shirts) come from [DummyJSON](https://dummyjson.com), a free public test API for demo/prototyping use. Re-fetch with `.venv/bin/python fetch_catalog.py`. Product images remain the property of their original owners; check DummyJSON's terms before using them in a public product.

More free sample items come from [Fake Store API](https://fakestoreapi.com), also a free test API. Same caveat: images belong to their owners, so replace them before any public launch.
