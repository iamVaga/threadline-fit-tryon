# Image credits

FitCheck uses original vector garment illustrations for the synthetic catalogue. They are navigation aids. Live try-on requires a garment-only photo mapped to the exact SKU or an upload of a flat-lay or mannequin photograph that the operator may use.

The Wikimedia photos from the previous demo were removed. No photographs or render examples are bundled. A gallery image needs explicit public display permission in addition to consent to process the source photo.
