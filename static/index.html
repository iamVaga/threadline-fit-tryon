How to measure

Bust: around the fullest part of your chest, tape level across your back. Waist: around the narrowest part of your torso. Hip: around the fullest part of your hips. Keep the tape snug but not tight, and measure over thin clothing. Kurtas are sized to clear your fullest measurement, so hip and bust matter most.
